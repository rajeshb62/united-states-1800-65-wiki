United States 1800–65 Wiki

A narrative-research knowledge base on American history from the turn of the nineteenth century through the end of the Civil War — Jeffersonian and Madisonian republicanism, the War of 1812 and the postwar settlement, the market and transportation revolutions, Jacksonian democracy, westward expansion, the deepening sectional crisis over slavery, and the war for the Union through 1865.

What follows is the map of the wiki — people, events, and concepts — then the source library on which every claim rests. Entity, event, and concept pages are built from those sources under a contemporary lens, without modern editorial framing.
