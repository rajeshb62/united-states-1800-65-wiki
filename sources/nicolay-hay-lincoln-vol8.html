Overview

Volume Eight covers late 1863–mid 1864: Northern secret societies and conspiracies; habeas corpus; Rosecrans’s march to Chattanooga, Chickamauga, and the relief and battles of Chattanooga; Burnside in East Tennessee; Lincoln’s Gettysburg Address; Missouri radical–conservative conflict; Meade on the Rapidan; foreign relations in 1863 (rams, France); Olustee and the Red River; the Pomeroy circular and Chase candidacy; Grant as general-in-chief; the Wilderness, Spotsylvania, and Cold Harbor; and free-state reconstruction movements in Arkansas, Louisiana, Tennessee, Maryland, and Missouri.
