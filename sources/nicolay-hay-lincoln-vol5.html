Overview

Volume Five covers the first full campaign year of the war: Hatteras and Port Royal and the Trent affair; the Tennessee and East Tennessee lines; Halleck, Cameron–Stanton, and Lincoln’s pressure for cooperation; Grant and Thomas in Kentucky and Fort Donelson; compensated abolishment proposals; Monitor and Merrimac; Roanoke Island; Farragut and New Orleans; Pea Ridge and Island No. 10; Shiloh and Halleck’s Corinth campaign; Yorktown through Fair Oaks; Stonewall Jackson’s Valley campaign; the Seven Days; and Harrison’s Landing.
